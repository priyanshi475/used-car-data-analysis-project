# 🚗 Used Car Sales EDA

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** of a Used Car Sales dataset.  
The goal is to analyze different factors that influence the **selling price of used cars** and identify key patterns in the market.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Understand factors affecting car prices
- Identify popular car brands and categories
- Analyze impact of mileage on price
- Study price variation based on:
  - Body type
  - Drive type
  - Manufacturing year
- Help businesses and customers make better decisions

---

## 📊 Dataset Features
The dataset includes:
- Selling Price
- Mileage
- Brand & Model
- Manufacturing Year
- Body Type
- Number of Cylinders
- Drive Type
- Engine Details
- Location (Zipcode)

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Certain brands dominate the used car market
- Mileage negatively impacts price
- Newer cars have higher selling prices
- SUVs and Sedans are more common
- Drive type influences pricing trends

---

## 💡 Business Impact
- Helps sellers price cars correctly
- Helps buyers make informed decisions
- Useful for inventory planning in car businesses
